Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Palm City Intel ("the App") is a private tool used only by the channel owner to publish videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Data we access

With the owner's authorization, the App accesses basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) and the permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account. No data of other users is collected.

How data is used

Access tokens are used only to upload and publish the owner's videos. They are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Google account settings. On revocation, locally stored tokens are deleted.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact Palm City Intel (@palmcityintel) via TikTok or YouTube.
